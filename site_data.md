# site_data.md

## 1. https://www.dasny.org/
**Description:**
The Dormitory Authority of the State of New York (DASNY) is a public-benefit corporation serving as New York State’s primary authority for the financing, design, and construction of public and non-profit infrastructure (such as higher education, healthcare, and public-purpose facilities). 

**Data it contains:**
*   **Financial Data:** Information on debt issuance, bonds, pricing, and audited financial statements.
*   **Construction & Project Information:** Details on ongoing and active construction/renovation projects, CAD/BIM standards, safety documentation, and state capital grant programs.
*   **Procurement & Opportunities:** Requests for Proposals (RFPs), active bid opportunities for contractors and professional service providers, and MWBE/SDVOB (Minority and Women-owned Business Enterprises) participation data.
*   **Governance Records:** Board members, organizational structure, FOIL (Freedom of Information Law) requests, and policies.

## 2. https://dallascityhall.bonfirehub.com/portal/?tab=openOpportunities
**Description:**
This is the official Procurement Portal for the City of Dallas, hosted on the Bonfire e-procurement platform. It facilitates the public procurement process, allowing vendors to view and bid on opportunities with the city government.

**Data it contains:**
*   **Procurement Opportunities:** Detailed listings of Open Opportunities, Past (historical) Opportunities, and Auctions.
*   **Contract Information:** Public Contracts data, including status (Pending, Active, Expired, Terminated), vendor names, start and end dates, and whether contracts are extendable.
*   **Project Details:** Reference numbers, relevant departments (e.g., Dallas Water Utilities, Public Works), commodity codes, and countdowns ("Days Left") for open bids.
*   **Vendor and Registration Data:** Resources for vendor registration, MWBE (Minority and Women Business Enterprise) tracking, and proposal submissions.

## 3. https://jwiz.com/
**Description:**
JWiz is a digital Jewish Directory and community resource platform that has been connecting businesses to the Jewish community since 1989. It functions similarly to a specialized local business directory and classifieds portal.

**Data it contains:**
*   **Business Listings:** A comprehensive "Kosher Directory" and "Jewish Directory" of local businesses, categorized for easy browsing and searching.
*   **Promotional Content:** Hot deals, coupons, and business cards for advertised services.
*   **Community Resources:** Local Jewish news, a Jewish calendar, classified ads, and a Jewish Referral Service.
*   **Advertising Data:** Options and forms for business owners to advertise, list their services, and access business-focused tools.

## 4. https://www.nyscr.ny.gov/
**Description:**
The New York State Contract Reporter (NYSCR) is the official publication of contracting opportunities for New York State. It serves to bring businesses and government entities together by publishing state procurement opportunities.

**Data it contains:**
*   **Bid and Contract Opportunities:** Searchable database to "Find Bids" across various New York State agencies and departments. Real-time counts of opportunities posted today, in the past 7 days, and total open opportunities.
*   **Business Registry:** A database where businesses can register to be notified of relevant opportunities.
*   **Educational / Guidance Data:** Resources on "Doing Business with NYS", policies, disclaimers, and links to related state systems (like VendRep, SFS, and Empire State Development).
*   **Advertising Portal:** A section for state and local government agencies to "Advertise Bids" and manage their postings.
